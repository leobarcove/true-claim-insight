How a claim moves through the platform

Eleven flows covering a claimant's first notification through to the fee note — the intake funnel, the regulated engagement, and the clocks running underneath both. Every state machine here is transcribed from the running code, so a diagram and the server disagreeing is a defect in one of them, not a matter of interpretation.

Four intake channels converge on a single Case funnel. A vetted Case converts into a Claim, which is the regulated engagement — and where the firm's professional obligations attach.

The firm recommends; the insurer decides. Nothing in this system settles a claim. Step 7 is a handback, and the adjuster report says so on its face.

Step 4 is not built. The assessment-mode router and its desk-review fast-track are specified in MASTER_PLAN.md §2.4 but exist nowhere in the codebase — no AssessmentMode field, no routing logic. Today a claim proceeds to assessment without a mode being selected for it.

03

A claimant submits a case

Conversational intake: one question at a time, resumable through currentStepId, with the question set chosen by travel claim type. 

sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor CL as Claimant
    participant PWA as Claimant PWA
    participant GW as API Gateway
    participant CS as case-service
    participant ST as Storage

    CL->>PWA: Start a claim
    PWA->>GW: POST /auth/claimant/send-otp
    GW-->>CL: OTP by SMS
    CL->>PWA: Enter OTP
    PWA->>GW: POST /auth/claimant/verify-otp
    GW-->>PWA: JWT — sub IS the claimant id

    PWA->>CL: Show PDPA notice, EN / BM
    CL->>PWA: Consent
    PWA->>CS: POST /consent — against approved wording v1

    PWA->>CS: POST /cases with travelClaimType
    CS-->>PWA: Case DRAFT + first step

    loop One step at a time
        PWA->>CL: Ask step prompt
        CL->>PWA: Answer
        PWA->>CS: PATCH /cases/:id/answers
        CS->>CS: validateAnswer against the flow rule
        alt bank account number
            CS->>CS: encrypt, mask in answers, last4 in the clear
        end
        CS-->>PWA: next step + completeness
    end

    loop Required documents
        CL->>PWA: Upload evidence
        PWA->>CS: POST /cases/:id/documents
        CS->>ST: store file
        CS->>CS: stamp documentsCompleteAt on the last mandatory item
    end

    CL->>PWA: Review and submit
    PWA->>CS: POST /cases/:id/submit
    CS->>CS: policy auto-match, then CSP 24h / 30-day flags
    CS-->>CL: Case SUBMITTED — reference number

Two flags are computed at submission, both advisory. notifiedLate marks notification more than 24 hours after the incident; outOfWindow marks more than 30 days. They surface as warnings to the operator and never auto-reject — the deadline is the insurer's to enforce, not the intake form's.

05

Insurer-appointed entry — the Assignment

Under TPA operation a Case converts into a Claim. Under insurer appointment the Assignment comes first and the Case is optional — this is where the regulated engagement actually begins. 

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> RECEIVED: insurer instructs the firm

    RECEIVED --> ACKNOWLEDGED: ack sent
    RECEIVED --> DECLINED: conflict or capacity

    ACKNOWLEDGED --> ACCEPTED: claim opened
    ACKNOWLEDGED --> DECLINED

    ACCEPTED --> COMPLETED: closed out

    DECLINED --> [*]
    COMPLETED --> [*]

    note right of RECEIVED
        Starts the ACK_TO_INSURER clock:
        one working day, per CSP.
        Before Assignment existed the clock
        measured from nothing, so it could be
        neither met nor missed.
    end note

    note right of ACCEPTED
        Registered mode gates here: conflict
        declared, competency matched,
        rotation checked.
    end note

The acknowledgement is now sent, not just recorded. Acknowledging emails the appointing contact and stops the CSP clock in the same act, so the one-working-day obligation is discharged by the system rather than by remembering.

    note right of CLOSED
        The reopen edge exists because the
        supplementary endpoint really does
        reopen, and starts a five-working-day
        CSP clock. The machine tells the truth

    end note

    note left of REPORT_PENDING

Three statuses exist but nothing reaches them. DOCUMENTS_PENDING, PENDING_ASSIGNMENT and UNDER_REVIEW are defined in ClaimStatus and shown above detached, because no transition leads to any of them. A recorded Phase 1 lifecycle gap, pinned by a test so it cannot be quietly forgotten.

Segregation of duties. The assessor cannot decide their own claim unless an AuthorityLimit expressly permits it, and never above its monetary ceiling. The basis is written onto the audit row, so the question "on whose authority?" has an answer years later.

08

The CSP clocks running underneath

Turnaround obligations run in parallel with the workflow. The firm's own stages escalate; the insurer's stages are measured but never escalated against the firm. 

flowchart LR
    subgraph FIRM["The firm's turnaround — escalated"]
        direction TB
        A["`ACK_TO_INSURER
1 working day`"]
        P["`PRELIMINARY_REPORT
per-insurer target`"]
        F["`FINAL_REPORT
10 working days from
complete documents`"]
        S["`SUPPLEMENTARY_CLAIM
5 working days`"]
        A --> P --> F
        F -.-> S
    end

    subgraph INSURER["Insurer side — measured, never escalated"]
        direction TB
        D["`INSURER_DECISION
7 working days`"]
        Y["`INSURER_PAYMENT
14 working days`"]
        D --> Y
    end

    F --> D

    SWEEP["Sweep every 15 minutes"] --> BR{"Past due?"}
    BR -->|yes| ESC["`BREACHED
level 1 → 2 at 2 working days
→ 3 at 5`"]
    ESC --> CE["`Level 3 raises a ComplianceEvent
PD 11.2 d — Board escalation`"]
    BR -->|"due soon"| WARN["One warning per clock"]

    style D stroke:#5C7FCB,stroke-width:2.5px
    style Y stroke:#5C7FCB,stroke-width:2.5px
    style CE stroke:#17966A,stroke-width:2.5px

The escalation now reaches a person. A firm-owned breach emails the operations address, deduplicated per clock and per level so a breach left open over a weekend sends once rather than every fifteen minutes. Insurer-side stages are measured and never escalated against the firm, so they send nothing.

The row is written before parsing, so an email nobody could understand still leaves a trace. A claimant who emailed you believes they have notified you — silently dropped intake is invisible, because nobody goes looking for a claim that was never created.

10

Small-claims fast-track

Four conditions must all hold before a claim takes the desk-review route, and any one of three triggers pulls it back out mid-flight.

None of this is built. This is the design recorded in MASTER_PLAN.md §2.4, drawn so the intended rules are reviewable — not a description of current behaviour. There is no fast-track logic, no tenant limit configuration and no mode field in the schema. 

flowchart TD
    IN["Claim opened"] --> C1{"`Category in the tenant's
fast-track list?`"}
    C1 -->|no| STD["Standard mode"]
    C1 -->|yes| C2{"`Estimated amount within
the tenant's limit?`"}
    C2 -->|no| STD
    C2 -->|yes| C3{"`Any open fraud signal
at MEDIUM or above?`"}
    C3 -->|yes| STD
    C3 -->|no| C4{"`Evidence checklist
complete?`"}
    C4 -->|no| STD
    C4 -->|yes| FAST["`**DESK_REVIEW**
fast-track SLA · single adjuster
short-form report`"]

    FAST --> TRIG{"`Escalation trigger
mid-flight?`"}
    TRIG -->|"`fraud flag · amount revised up ·
extraction inconsistency`"| UP["`Escalate one level:
video → site → expert`"]
    TRIG -->|none| OUT["Complete on fast-track"]
    UP --> STD

    style FAST stroke:#C77A2E,stroke-width:2.5px,stroke-dasharray:5 4
    style OUT stroke:#C77A2E,stroke-width:2.5px,stroke-dasharray:5 4

Mode changes are audited and disclosed in the report's methodology section. The assessment mode is a method under PD 12.6, and a reader is entitled to know which one produced the figure.

11

Who may do what

Roles are tenant-scoped. Adjusting firms and insurers are separate tenants, and a record belonging to another tenant is indistinguishable from one that does not exist.
